Questions

Everything about the Mira smart mirror.

What is Mira?

Mira is an AI smart mirror — a voice-controlled mirror powered by Claude AI that shows your calendar, weather, WhatsApp messages and music, coaches your workouts, and recognises your face to load your personal dashboard automatically.

How does the Mira smart mirror work?

Say "Hey Mira" to wake it. The mirror listens with an always-on wake word, understands your request with on-device Claude AI, and responds in under two seconds — hands-free. It runs on a Raspberry Pi behind a two-way mirror and connects to your Google Calendar, Spotify and WhatsApp.

Is the Mira smart mirror private and secure?

Yes. Mira's camera has an indicator LED that lights up when active, can be disabled anytime from the companion app, and footage never leaves your device — there is no cloud subscription and no cloud storage.

When will Mira ship and how much does it cost?

The first batch is being built in Pune, India, and ships in 2026 in limited units. Join the waitlist for early access and founding-member pricing.

What can I control with my voice on Mira?

You can ask about your day, play or pause music, get the weather, check messages, start and control workouts, launch karaoke mode, and more — all completely hands-free.
